Runs 100% On Your Device

Ever wondered what is actually happening when you talk to ChatGPT or Gemini?

When you chat with AI, it feels like talking to a person who plans out whole sentences in their head. It doesn't! Under the hood, AI is essentially a super-powered autocomplete game—predicting strictly one word at a time. This app slows a real AI down to human speed so you can watch how it works.

🧩
1. Chop Words (Tokenization)

AI chops your sentence into numbered puzzle pieces called tokens.

🔦
2. Find Clues (Self-Attention)

The AI connects words together to lock onto grammar and topic rules.

📊
3. Score Words (Logits / Softmax)

The AI holds a contest across its dictionary and gives candidate words percentage odds.

🎲
4. Roll the Dice (Sampling)

Instead of picking the #1 word every time, it rolls a weighted die for creativity.

🧠
Why is this AI a little goofy? ("Pocket Brain" vs. Supercomputers)

Big AI tools like ChatGPT and Google Gemini run on giant data-center supercomputers. So that your own device can run this live without melting, this page loads a tiny, miniature AI brain. It isn't nearly as smart, but the step-by-step guessing game it plays is 100% identical to the big ones!
