Demystifying GenAI

Have you ever wanted to know EXACTLY what is happening when you interface with generative AI?

Whether you use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or local models, they all share the exact same mathematical loop. This interactive sandbox brings the machine down to human speed using a real in-browser neural network.

🧩
1. Tokenization

AI doesn't read letters. It converts your prompt into specific numerical IDs from its dictionary.

🔦
2. Attention & Context

Every token references every other token to weigh context and determine meaning.

📊
3. Logits & Odds

The AI does not write sentences; it ranks candidates from its dictionary for the next slot.

🎲
4. Temperature & Sampling

Control the randomness of the selection before picking the winning token and looping.
