Plain-English Guide Runs 100% On Your Device

Ever wondered what is actually happening when you talk to ChatGPT or Gemini?

When you chat with AI, it feels like talking to a person who plans out whole sentences in their head. It doesn't! Under the hood, AI is essentially a super-powered autocomplete game—guessing just one word at a time. This app slows a real AI down to human speed so you can watch how it works.

🧩
1. Word Puzzle Pieces ("Tokens")

AI can't read letters like we do. It chops your sentence into numbered puzzle pieces called tokens.

🔦
2. Connecting Clues

The AI scans all the words in your question at once to figure out the topic and grammar rules.

📊
3. The Word Contest

To write the very next word, the AI holds a contest across its dictionary and gives the best-fitting words a percentage score.

🎲
4. Rolling the Dice

Instead of picking the #1 most obvious word every time, AI rolls a weighted die so its answers feel fresh and creative.

🧠
Why is this AI a little goofy? ("Pocket Brain" vs. Supercomputers)

Big AI tools like ChatGPT and Google Gemini run on giant data-center supercomputers. So that your own phone or computer can run this live without melting, this page loads a tiny, miniature AI brain. It isn't nearly as smart, but the step-by-step guessing game it plays is 100% identical to the big ones!

Privacy & Independence: Everything runs privately inside your web browser. This is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), or Anthropic (Claude).
