Interactive AI Deconstructor 100% Client-Side Neural Net

Ever wondered what is actually happening inside ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude?

When you chat with frontier AI systems, it feels like talking to a mind that plans out paragraphs. It isn't. Every Large Language Model (LLM) on Earth runs on the exact same mathematical loop—predicting strictly one token at a time. This sandbox slows a real neural network down to human speed so you can see inside the black box.

🧩
1. Subword Tokenization

AI doesn't see letters or words. It slices your prompt into numerical IDs from a fixed dictionary before doing any math.

🔦
2. Self-Attention & Context

Every word calculates mathematical connections to prior words to lock onto grammar, tone, and topic constraints.

📊
3. The Logit Election

For every single blank slot, the model scores its entire vocabulary. Watch the top candidates compete in real time.

🎲
4. Temperature & Timeline Forking

Drag the Temperature slider to reshape the odds live, or click any generated word to rewrite history down a new branch.

Educational Notice: The transformer architecture shown here (Tokenization → Self-Attention → Logit Softmax → Autoregressive Sampling) is the exact mathematical foundation used by OpenAI's ChatGPT, Google's Gemini, and Anthropic's Claude, scaled down to run privately inside your browser.
