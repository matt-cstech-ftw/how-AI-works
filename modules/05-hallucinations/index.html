The 30-Second Lesson: Massive Internet Datasets & Bias

How does an AI learn millions of word probabilities and star coordinates? During a phase called Pre-Training, giant AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini digest massive datasets collected from across the public internet—billions of web pages, books, articles, code repositories, and forum discussions.

The Catch ("Garbage In, Garbage Out"): Because the internet contains human arguments, spam, conspiracy theories, and offensive language, an unfiltered AI will absorb those exact flaws and repeat them! Engineers must build high-speed filters to feed the model Clean Data while blasting away Toxic & Corrupted Data.
