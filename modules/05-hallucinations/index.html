Aha! You Just Explored the Entire AI Pipeline in Reverse!

Notice what we did across these 5 modules? When you put them in forward order, you have the complete end-to-end process of how AI understands you and writes a response:

1. THE DATA DIET (LAB 05) Digesting the Internet

First, the AI learns language patterns by training on billions of curated web pages, books, and articles.

2. WORD SLICER (LAB 04) Chopping into Tokens

When you type a prompt, the tokenizer slices your words into numbered subword puzzle pieces.

3. STAR MAP (LAB 03) Mapping Your Meaning

Your tokens light up as coordinates in vector space, finding the center of gravity of your thought.

4. SELF-ATTENTION (LAB 02) Connecting the Clues

Words fire connection beams across the sentence so pronouns and context stay locked in.

5. WORD CONTEST (LAB 01) Guessing Word-by-Word

Finally, the model scores its dictionary and elects one piece at a time to build your response!
